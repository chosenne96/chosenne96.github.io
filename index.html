I'm currently a Master's Candidate in the Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences at Seoul National University (SNU), South Korea, working under the supervision of Prof. SangAh Lee.

My research aims to understand the mechanisms of human learning and memory through spatial-based paradigms — investigating how the hippocampus resolves representational ambiguity and integrates novel information into existing memory frameworks.

About

Research Interests

I aspire to study spatial cognition and its underlying neural mechanisms. By integrating experimental behavior, neuroimaging, and computational modeling, I seek to access human cognition in a mechanistic way.

Research Questions

  • How do we integrate and represent spatial information?
  • When do we complete our mental representations of space?
  • What factors contribute to efficient spatial learning?
