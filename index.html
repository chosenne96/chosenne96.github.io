I'm currently a Master's Candidate in the Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences at Seoul National University (SNU), South Korea. I have the fantastic opportunity to work under the supervision of Prof. SangAh Lee.

My research aims to understand the mechanisms of human learning and memory through spatial-based paradigms. Specifically, I am interested in investigating how the hippocampus resolves representational ambiguity and integrates novel information into existing memory frameworks and how these neural codes guide behavior. By integrating experimental behavior, neuroimaging, and computational modeling, I seek to access human cognition in a mechanistic way.
